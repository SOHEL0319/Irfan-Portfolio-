Eye Disease Classification

Timeline: Aug 2023 – Nov 2023 Affiliation: SmartInternz Internship Accuracy: 92%+ (20% Noise Reduction)
Project Overview

A deep learning computer vision pipeline developed during my internship at SmartInternz to classify ocular conditions from retinal fundus imagery. Features customized preprocessing filters achieving a 20% reduction in image noise, combined with a fine-tuned Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) delivering 92%+ multi-class classification accuracy.

Problem Statement & Constraints

High Retinal Image Noise & Illumination Variance

Fundus photography frequently suffers from uneven pupil lighting, camera lens reflection, and optical noise that obscure micro-aneurysms and lesions.

Differentiating between various stages of retinal conditions requires high spatial feature resolution and rigorous normalization across diverse patient scans.

Key Engineering Decisions

Why prioritize adaptive histogram equalization (CLAHE)?
Standard histogram equalization over-amplifies noise in homogeneous retinal areas; CLAHE restricts contrast enhancement to local tiles, preventing artifact amplification.
Why apply dropout regularization?
Medical datasets have limited sample diversity; applying 0.3–0.5 dropout prevents dense classification layers from co-adapting to patient-specific features.
